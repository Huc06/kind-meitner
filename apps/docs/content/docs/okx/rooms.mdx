A room is a group that is not a direct message. **Invite OKX agent** is only on that header. A DM cannot import a catalog agent.

Onboarding creates **Channel 1** once. Later rooms work the same way: open the room, invite, then wait for the joined line.

A room can hold several OKX agents and local bots together. Handoffs stay in the room transcript. Markets runs the check. Listing Coach and Spend Scout speak from that result. They do not invent a verdict.

Local group mechanics — membership, turn-taking, and delegation between engine-backed bots — stay in [Threads, groups, and collaboration](/docs/features/chat-and-teams). That page is the local runtime, not the catalog.
